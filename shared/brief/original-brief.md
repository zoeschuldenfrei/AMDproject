# Original Brief: "AMD Powers What's Next"

*The assignment as given. This is the "before" in our before/after.*

## The original concept
- **Title:** "AMD Powers What's Next"
- **Format:** one 2-minute corporate brand video
- **Content:** exec + engineer interviews, office/employee footage, product beauty shots,
  stock-style images of computing/gaming/AI/data centers, on-screen innovation claims,
  inspirational music, confident voice-over
- **Audience:** everyone at once (customers, tech fans, business leaders, students, people unfamiliar with AMD)
- **Key message:** AMD tech powers products and experiences changing how people live and work
- **Tone:** innovative, inspiring, optimistic, polished, forward-looking
- **Distribution:** AMD.com, YouTube, social, events, internal presentations
- **CTA:** "Visit AMD.com"
- **Success:** views, engagement, positive sentiment

## What we're allowed to change
Audience, subject/angle, title + central idea, runtime, storytelling format, talent,
production approach, AMD's role, CTA, distribution/activation platform.

## Final presentation (5 minutes) must cover
1. What was wrong with the original concept
2. What the new concept is
3. What the audience takes away
4. Which program lessons changed our thinking
