# ZENITH: Sizzle Reel Scripts & Storyboards

**Rules every reel follows**
1. **The hook comes in the first second.** The first frame has to work with the sound off.
2. **Each reel works alone.** Someone who never watches the show should still enjoy it.
3. **One district, one character, one idea per reel.**
4. **Vertical first (9:16)**, with a 16:9 cutdown for YouTube and events where noted.
5. **Captions burned in** (most social viewing starts muted).
6. **The logo appears only at the end** (on the city's infrastructure or the end card), never in the first 3 seconds.
7. **End card:** `ZENITH · Find the power.` + small AMD logo + "Ep 1 on YouTube."

| # | Title | Length | Platform(s) | Phase | Job |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The City Under Your Screen | 30s | TikTok, Reels, Shorts | Tease | Introduce the world |
| 2 | Meet Pix | 20s | TikTok, Reels | Tease | Character attachment and a meme-able mascot |
| 3 | Every Time You Hit Send | 45s | TikTok, Reels, Shorts | Launch | Reveal the data-center Undercity (AMD's least-known side) |
| 4 | The Flicker | 15s | All + pre-roll | Launch | Stakes and urgency, pushes viewers to Ep 1 |
| 5 | Enter the Zenith Open | 60s | YouTube (16:9) + Shorts + Twitch | Sustain | Creator competition recruitment |

## Production notes
- **Reuse:** Reels 1, 2 and 4 are cut almost entirely from series animation. Reel 3 needs about 15s of
  extra animation. Reel 5 mixes series footage with creator-supplied clips. This keeps the cost per reel low.
- **Ongoing pipeline after launch** (2–4 reels per episode):
  - "Pix asks..." Shorts (one AMD use case each)
  - Logo-count reveals after each episode
  - District "postcards" (6s looping wallpapers, made for saves)
  - Character POV memes
  - Behind-the-scenes: "How we rendered Zenith" (where animation and rendering hardware can legitimately feature)
- **Testing:** A/B test two hooks per reel in the first 48 hours (text hook vs. sound hook) and scale the one with
  the better 3-second hold.
