# Audience, CTA, Distribution & Success Metrics

## 1. New audience

### Primary: "Digital-native explorers," ages 16–28
Gamers, animation and anime fans, content creators, and students curious about tech, design or
engineering careers. They:
- Use AMD-powered things every day (consoles, laptops, cloud apps) without knowing it.
- Skip ads in under 2 seconds but will binge an 8-minute episode about a world they like.
- Treat fandom as participation: theories, fan art, stitches, Discord, streams.
- Don't trust brand claims. They do trust creators and stories.

**Persona: "Maya, 19."** A second-year college student. She games most nights, watched *Arcane*
twice, and edits TikToks on her laptop. She knows AMD as "a chip thing for PC builders." After
Zenith, she knows it's in her console, her group chat's servers, the AI tools she uses, and the
hospital scanner that checked her knee.

### Secondary audiences (reached *through* the primary, not targeted separately)
| Audience | How Zenith reaches them |
|---|---|
| **Creators and streamers** | The Zenith Open gives them content, a stage, and prizes |
| **Future talent** (students in tech and art) | Kit's story, the student animation contest, and the "how we made it" reels position AMD as a place creative and technical people want to work |
| **Business and tech decision-makers** | They see the cultural buzz, and the 16:9 series and Observatory/Undercity episodes play at events and in sales decks as proof of breadth |
| **Internal employees** | They see their own work turned into a city district, which makes them proud and likely to share |

**Why narrow is better here:** The original tried to talk to everyone in one video. We talk to one
audience that *spreads* things. Fans share, and their sharing reaches the others.

## 3. New CTA
### Master CTA: **"Find the power."**
It works as a tagline, a game mechanic and an instruction at once. Every placement points to a
specific action, never just "visit a website":

| Where | CTA | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Sizzle reels | *Find the power. Ep 1 on YouTube* | Watch the series |
| Episode end cards | *Find the power. Explore the district →* | Open the interactive Zenith map on AMD.com, where each district links to the real AMD product story |
| Logo-hunt posts | *Find the power. How many did you spot?* | Comment, rewatch |
| Zenith Open | *Find the power. Enter the Open →* | Sign up to compete, create or stream |
| Student contest | *Find the power. Build your district →* | Submit a short film or district design |

**Compared with the original:** "Visit AMD.com" gave people nowhere specific to go and nothing to do. "Find the power" gives
them a reason (curiosity), a destination (the map) and something to do (hunt, play, create).

## 4. Distribution and activation plan

| Phase | Timing | What goes out | Channels |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Tease** | Weeks -3 to 0 | Reels 1 and 2. Mystery posts ("There's a city inside your console"). No series announcement until week -1. | TikTok, Reels, Shorts, X/Threads |
| **2. Launch** | Week 0 | Ep 1 + 2 premiere. Reels 3 and 4. Interactive map goes live. Pre-roll (Reel 4) on gaming and animation channels. 10–15 seeded creators react live. | YouTube premiere, AMD.com hub, paid social, creator seeding |
| **3. Sustain** | Weeks 1–4 | One episode a week (Ep 3–6). 2–4 reels per episode. Logo-count reveals. Discord community. Zenith Open entries open (Reel 5). Student contest opens. | All social + Discord + Twitch |
| **4. Live finale** | Week 5 | Zenith Open finals streamed live from an event stage (e.g., a gaming or tech convention or an AMD keynote). Ep 6 premieres right after the finals. | Twitch / YouTube Live, event screens |
| **5. Afterlife** | Ongoing | Full season on YouTube and AMD.com. 16:9 cuts for events, sales decks and internal town halls. Winning fan district gets developed for Season 2. | Evergreen; events; internal |

### The Zenith Open (creator competition)
- **What:** A competition inside a Zenith map built on an existing game/creation platform (a
  partner game or user-generated-content platform, which avoids building a game from scratch).
- **Three tracks:**
  1. **🎮 PLAY:** speedrun the city and hunt hidden AMD logos for bonus points.
  2. **🎨 CREATE:** design a new district for a real-world area AMD powers. The winner's district gets animated into Season 2.
  3. **📡 STREAM:** best live run, voted on by the audience.
- **Prizes:** AMD-powered gaming and creator setups, a spot at the live finals, and a credit in Season 2.
- **Why it works:** Creators make the content, which turns a one-way broadcast into hundreds of channels.

### Where the original's channels still fit
| Original channel | New role |
|---|---|
| AMD.com | Becomes the series hub and interactive map, a place to go for a reason |
| YouTube | Home of the series (not a dumping ground for a single brand video) |
| Social | Sizzle reels made for each platform |
| Events | Live finals and 16:9 episode screenings |
| Internal | Season screenings at town halls, employee "find your district" posts |

## 5. Success metrics

The original measured **views and sentiment**. We measure **attention → attachment → action → recall.**

| Stage | Metric | Why it matters | Example target* |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Attention** | 3-second hold rate (reels) | Shows whether the hook works | ≥ 60% |
| | Reel watch-through / completion rate | Whether people stay | ≥ 35% (30s+ reels) |
| **Attachment** | Episode completion rate | Whether the story holds people | ≥ 60% per episode |
| | Episode-to-episode retention (Ep 1 → Ep 6) | Whether they come back | ≥ 40% of Ep 1 viewers finish Ep 6 |
| | Follows gained on AMD channels during the campaign | Lasting reach beyond one video | Track lift vs. baseline |
| | Saves and shares per 1K views | Saves and shares predict organic spread better than likes do | Beat AMD's channel average by 2× |
| **Action** | Interactive map visits and time on page | Whether the CTA works | Avg. 2+ districts explored per visit |
| | Zenith Open entries (per track) | Creator participation | e.g., 1,000+ entries, 100+ streamers |
| | UGC volume (#Zenith, #FindThePower, fan art, stitches, theories) | Fandom forming | Track weekly growth |
| | Discord members | Community depth | Track growth |
| **Recall** | **Brand-lift study:** aided and unaided recall of AMD | The metric that answers the original brief | Significant lift vs. control |
| | **"Breadth" association:** % who link AMD with AI, data centers, healthcare and automotive (not just gaming/PCs) | Our core goal: knowing AMD's range | e.g., +15 pts on non-gaming sectors |
| | Comment sentiment and theme analysis | *What* people say, not just whether it's positive | Mentions of "didn't know AMD did that" |

*\*Targets are illustrative for the pitch. The real ones would be set against AMD's channel baselines.*

**The headline metric for the deck:** Before Zenith, ask "What does AMD power?" and people say "PC
chips." After Zenith, they say "the whole city."
