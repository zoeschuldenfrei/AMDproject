# ZENITH: World Bible

> *A city that never sleeps, never lags, and never stops to tell you who keeps the lights on.*

## 1. The big idea
Zenith is a futuristic megacity where every system (the arenas, the AI, the hospitals,
the trains, the internet itself) runs on one shared, living power grid. Citizens call it
**the Current**. Viewers who look closely work out that the Current *is* AMD.

**The rule of the world:** AMD is never a character who talks and never a corporation in the plot.
It's the infrastructure. The logo appears where a real city's power company or chip
maker would appear: on substations, server racks, arena scoreboards, transit cars, hospital
scanners. You find it. Nobody points it out.

**Why "Zenith"?** It means the highest point, which fits a city of peak performance. It also hides
**"Zen"**, the name of AMD's CPU architecture, so tech fans get an Easter egg on day one.

## 2. City layout: a city shaped like a chip
Seen from above, Zenith is laid out like a modern processor package. Each district is a
**chiplet**, a self-contained block with its own job, and they're connected by glowing elevated
bridges the citizens call **the Fabric**. This is a visual nod to AMD's chiplet design and
Infinity Fabric interconnect. Engineers will spot it, and everyone else will just see a beautiful map.

                 │ OBSERVATORY  │  (supercomputing / science)

## 3. Districts

Each district has an AMD sector, a **look**, a **sound**, and a **"hidden world" detail**, the
Roku City-style wink where everyday tech turns out to be a living place.

### ⚡ The Core (the power grid)
- **AMD world:** The whole company, the shared foundation.
- **Look:** A colossal crystalline tower at the city's center, pulsing red-orange. The Current
  flows out of it along the Fabric like light through veins.
- **Sound:** A low, steady hum, like the city's heartbeat. When it skips, everyone notices.
- **Hidden-world detail:** Its pulse matches the rhythm of the city. It speeds up on game
  nights and slows down at 4 a.m.
- **The only place the AMD logo is shown in full,** on the Core's base, seen once per season.

### 🎮 The Glowline (gaming)
- **AMD world:** Gaming. Radeon graphics, Ryzen processors, and the custom chips inside major consoles.
- **Look:** A neon strip of stacked arenas, holographic scoreboards, food stalls and crowds. Saturated
  magenta, cyan and orange.
- **Sound:** Bass drops, crowd roars, the click-clack of controllers.
- **Hidden-world detail:** Every frame you see in a game is a tiny stage, rebuilt 144 times a
  second by an army of light-runners sprinting across it.
- **Home of:** Vee (lead), and the Zenith Open arena.

### 🧠 The Lattice (AI)
- **AMD world:** AI. Instinct accelerators, AI PCs, and AI at the edge.
- **Look:** Impossibly tall white-gold towers made of geometric scaffolding that rearranges
  itself as it "thinks." At night it looks like a constellation.
- **Sound:** Soft chimes, layered whispers, like a choir learning a song.
- **Hidden-world detail:** New AIs are "born" here as sparks that climb the towers as they learn.
- **Home of:** Pix.

### 🗄️ The Undercity (data centers)
- **AMD world:** Data centers and cloud (EPYC server processors). This is what lets the internet, streaming and
  messaging work.
- **Look:** A vast cavern under all of Zenith. Endless corridors of server-stacks like glowing
  library shelves, with cool blue light and drifting mist from cooling vents. Beautiful and cathedral-like.
- **Sound:** Rushing air, rhythmic clicks, distant echoes.
- **Hidden-world detail:** Every message, photo and stream in Zenith is a tiny lantern carried
  through these corridors. Hit "send" and one takes off.
- **Home of:** Marrow.

### 🏠 Homeblock (everyday PCs and creators)
- **AMD world:** Consumer and creator PCs (Ryzen laptops and desktops). Homework, editing,
  streaming, side hustles.
- **Look:** Cozy, dense, warm. Stacked apartments, rooftop gardens, bedroom studios glowing
  through windows. Warm amber and teal.
- **Sound:** Lo-fi beats drifting from windows, a kettle, keyboard clatter.
- **Hidden-world detail:** Inside every laptop is a tiny apartment building where the
  processes live. Open too many tabs and it gets *crowded*.
- **Home of:** Vee's family and Kit.

### 🩺 Pulse Quarter (healthcare)
- **AMD world:** Healthcare, meaning the processors and adaptive chips inside medical imaging and
  diagnostic equipment.
- **Look:** Calm, clean, soft greens and pearl white. Glass atriums, floating scan-light displays.
  The quietest district in the city.
- **Sound:** Soft synth pads and a heartbeat monitor that turns into music.
- **Hidden-world detail:** Scanners see the body as a glowing map of its own tiny city.
- **Home of:** Dr. Ines Calder.

### 🚄 The Loop (automotive, transit, embedded)
- **AMD world:** Automotive and embedded/adaptive computing (driver assistance, infotainment,
  industrial and robotics systems).
- **Look:** A circular elevated maglev ring with autonomous pods and cargo drones. Speed lines
  and motion blur, chrome and electric yellow.
- **Sound:** Whooshes, Doppler hums, rail chimes.
- **Hidden-world detail:** Every pod "sees" the city as a wireframe of moving shapes, and it never blinks.
- **Home of:** Juno.

### 🔭 The Observatory (supercomputing and science)
- **AMD world:** High-performance computing. AMD powers some of the world's fastest
  supercomputers, used for climate, energy and medical research.
- **Look:** A mountaintop dome above the clouds, with planets and weather systems simulated in midair.
- **Sound:** Orchestral swells, wind.
- **Hidden-world detail:** The Observatory simulates *tomorrow*, and it's where the Flicker is first detected.
- **Home of:** The Archivist.

## 4. Characters

All characters are original. None of them works "for AMD", because AMD is the world they live in.

### Vee Okoro, *the lead* (Glowline / Homeblock)
- **Age:** 19. **Pronouns:** she/her.
- **Who:** A rising arena gamer from Homeblock who's fast, cocky, and secretly terrified she's only
  good at one thing. She works as a courier on the Fabric to pay for arena entry fees.
- **Want:** To win the Zenith Open.
- **Need:** To see that the city (and she) is more than the arena.
- **Look:** Oversized jacket with reflective trim, one cybernetic glove that glows when she plays,
  braids threaded with fiber-optic beads.
- **Line:** *"Lag is a choice."*

### Pix, *the AI sidekick* (The Lattice)
- **Who:** A brand-new AI spark that escaped the Lattice before finishing "training." It's
  endlessly curious, takes things literally, and has never seen the sun. It lives in Vee's glove.
- **Role:** The audience surrogate. Pix asks "what is that?" so viewers learn about each district
  without exposition.
- **Look:** A small floating orb of shifting geometry. Its shape changes with its mood (cube when nervous,
  star when excited).
- **Line:** *"Is that what a sunrise is? Can we download another one?"*
- **Merch and social potential:** Very high. Pix is the reaction-GIF character.

### Marrow, *the keeper of the Undercity*
- **Who:** A gruff, sixty-something data-center engineer who's spent 30 years underground keeping
  the lanterns moving. Dry humor, very proud of work nobody sees.
- **Role:** Mentor. He represents the invisible-infrastructure theme in person.
- **Line:** *"Nobody thanks the plumbing. That's how you know it's working."*

### Dr. Ines Calder, *Pulse Quarter*
- **Who:** A calm, brilliant imaging specialist who sees patterns other people miss. She's the first to notice
  that the Flicker looks like a symptom.
- **Role:** Brings the story's emotional stakes. When the grid stutters, her scanners go dark mid-surgery.

### Juno Reyes, *The Loop*
- **Who:** A Loop pod pilot and underground racer, Vee's rival-turned-ally. Loud, loyal, fast.
- **Role:** Comic energy and action set-pieces.

### Kit Okoro, *Vee's younger brother* (Homeblock)
- **Who:** A 13-year-old animator making his first short film on a secondhand laptop.
- **Role:** The quiet heart of the story, showing that creativity is power too. He's the stand-in for every
  student in the audience.

### The Archivist, *The Observatory*
- **Who:** A mysterious, elegant figure who has run the city's simulations for decades. Ambiguous. Ally or threat?
- **Role:** Delivers the season's twist.

### The Flicker, *the antagonist force*
- **What:** A cascading glitch spreading across the Fabric that causes stutters, blackouts and corrupted
  data. It isn't a villain company. It's a *problem*, and the city can only solve it together.
- **Reveal (Ep 5):** The Flicker was caused by the districts being cut off from each other.
  The Archivist sealed the bridges to "protect" the Observatory. It's fixed when every district
  reconnects at the same moment.
- **Theme:** *It takes the whole stack.* This is AMD's breadth story, told as a plot.

## 5. Visual style guide

| Element | Direction |
|---|---|
| **Overall** | Stylized 2.5D. Painterly backgrounds with 3D characters, visible brushwork, cel-shaded lighting. Cinematic like *Arcane*, playful like Roku City. |
| **Palette** | Night city base (deep indigo #0E0B2B, charcoal). Each district has a signature accent (Glowline magenta, Lattice gold, Undercity ice blue, Homeblock amber, Pulse green, Loop yellow). **The Current is always AMD red-orange**, which makes it the most recognizable color in the world. |
| **Light** | Everything powered glows. When the Flicker hits, the glow drains to grey, so the audience *feels* what losing power means. |
| **Shapes** | Circuit-board geometry in the architecture: right angles, traces, pads. Districts read like chiplets from above. |
| **Type** | A wide geometric sans for titles. A monospaced "system" font for Pix's thoughts and on-screen HUDs. |
| **Logo use** | Never in dialogue. Appears on infrastructure (substations, racks, scoreboards, pod dashboards), with one full hero reveal on the Core per season. |
| **Camera** | Big sweeping flyovers for wonder. Tight handheld for character moments. "Zoom into the device" transitions for the Roku City hidden-world beat. |
| **Sound** | Each district has a musical motif. The Core's hum ties them together. The final episode layers every motif into one track. |

## 6. Real-world anchors (fact-check before presenting)
Each district is tied to a real AMD business. Before the pitch, confirm these against
AMD.com and recent press so the details are current:

- [ ] **Gaming:** Radeon GPUs and Ryzen CPUs. AMD makes the custom processors in PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
- [ ] **AI:** AMD Instinct accelerators for AI and data centers. Ryzen AI processors in AI PCs.
- [ ] **Data centers:** AMD EPYC server processors, used by major cloud providers.
- [ ] **PCs:** Ryzen processors in consumer and creator laptops and desktops.
- [ ] **Healthcare and embedded:** AMD adaptive and embedded processors (from the Xilinx acquisition) in medical imaging and industrial equipment.
- [ ] **Automotive:** AMD embedded and adaptive chips in driver-assist and in-vehicle systems.
- [ ] **Supercomputing:** AMD-powered systems such as Frontier and El Capitan have topped the TOP500 list.
- [ ] **Architecture names:** "Zen" (CPU architecture), chiplets, Infinity Fabric.

## 7. Room to grow
The world is built so it can keep expanding:
- **New product line = new district** (or a new building in an existing one).
- **Season 2 seeds:** The Undercity has levels nobody has mapped. Something is living down there.
- **Interactive:** A browsable Zenith map on AMD.com where each district links to the real product story.
- **Game layer:** A Zenith arena map inside an existing game platform for the creator competition.
