# Pitch Deck Outline: "Before → After" (5:00)

**Structure:** 10 slides. The four required beats map like this:

| Required beat | Slides | Time |
|---|---|---|
| 1. What was wrong with the original | 2–3 | 0:20–1:20 |
| 2. What the new concept is | 4–6 | 1:20–3:10 |
| 3. What the audience takes away | 7–8 | 3:10–4:00 |
| 4. Program lessons that changed our thinking | 9 | 4:00–4:40 |

Timings add up to **4:50**, which leaves 10 seconds of buffer. Rehearse with a stopwatch; slides 5 and 9 are the ones that run long.

**Design rule for the whole deck:** the "before" slides look like a corporate template (grey,
stock photos, centered bullets). At slide 4 the deck *transforms* into Zenith's look: dark city,
neon accents, character art. Without anyone saying it, the deck itself shows the before and after.

## Slide 3: What was wrong (0:45–1:20)
**Title:** Six reasons it would be scrolled past

| Problem | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| **Audience = everyone** | A message for customers, CEOs, gamers and students at once doesn't land with any of them. |
| **Tells instead of shows** | "Innovative" and "powering the future" are claims. Nobody shares a claim. |
| **Looks like every tech ad** | Execs, offices and glowing chips are the visual default of the whole category, so nothing about it says AMD. |
| **Wrong length for its channels** | Too long for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, and too generic to anchor an event. |
| **Passive CTA** | "Visit AMD.com" gives people no reason to go and nothing to do once they get there. |
| **Vanity metrics** | Views measure exposure. They don't tell you whether anyone remembers AMD or cares about it. |

**Say:** Pick 3 to say out loud (audience, tell-don't-show, CTA). Leave the rest on screen.
> "The core problem is that AMD's biggest strength is that it's *invisible*. It sits inside the
> console, the server and the hospital scanner, and the original video tried to fix that
> by talking louder."

**Say:**
> "Zenith is an animated miniseries. Think the cinematic, character-driven style of *Arcane*,

> power company would: on the infrastructure, everywhere, never in the dialogue."

## Slide 8: Audience, CTA and how we'd measure it (3:10–4:00)
**On screen:** Before/after table.

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| **Audience** | Everyone | Gen Z / young millennial digital natives (16–28): gamers, animation fans, creators, students curious about tech careers |
| **AMD's role** | Narrator | The city's infrastructure. It's the world, not the speaker |
| **Format** | One 2-min video | 6-episode miniseries + always-on reels + creator competition |
| **CTA** | "Visit AMD.com" | **"Find the power."** Explore the interactive Zenith map, watch the next episode, or enter the Zenith Open |
| **Success** | Views | Watch-through, follows, shares and saves, episode-to-episode retention, creator entries, **brand-lift recall** |

**Say:**
> "The takeaway we want is simple. If you ask someone after they've watched Zenith
> 'What does AMD power?', they say 'basically the whole city': games, AI, the internet,
> hospitals, cars. That's the metric that matters, and it's measurable with a brand-lift study."

## Backup slides (only if there's Q&A)
- **B1: Production approach.** A stylized 2.5D look keeps cost down: painted backgrounds with 3D
  characters, the way *Arcane* combines the two. Reels are cut from series assets, so every frame of
  animation gets used twice. Animation and rendering could themselves be shown off as running on AMD hardware
  (a behind-the-scenes reel).
- **B2: Phased rollout.** Tease (reels only) → Launch (Ep 1–2 + map) → Sustain (weekly eps +
  reels) → Zenith Open finals at a live event. Details in `05-audience-cta-distribution-metrics.md`.
- **B3: Why not just make a better 2-min video?** A single video is a one-off. A world can keep producing
  content for years: new districts for new product lines, new seasons, new creators.
- **B4: Risk, "is it too far from the product?"** Every district is tied to a real AMD sector,
  and the interactive map links each one to the real product. The story is there to get people curious enough to look.
