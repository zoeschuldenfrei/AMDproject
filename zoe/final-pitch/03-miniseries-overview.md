# ZENITH: Miniseries Overview

## Logline
> When a mysterious glitch called **the Flicker** starts blacking out a city that runs on a
> single living power grid, a cocky 19-year-old arena gamer and her runaway AI sidekick
> have to travel every district of Zenith, from the neon arenas to the data-center
> Undercity, to find the source before the whole city goes dark.

## Tagline options
- *Find the power.* ← recommended, and doubles as the CTA
- *The city never lags.*
- *Everything here runs on something.*

## Format

| | |
|---|---|
| **Type** | Animated miniseries, stylized 2.5D |
| **Season 1** | 6 episodes × ~8 minutes (about 48 minutes in total) |
| **Release** | Weekly. Ep 1 + 2 drop together at launch, then one per week |
| **Home** | YouTube (official AMD channel) and an embedded hub on AMD.com with the interactive map |
| **Companion** | 2–4 sizzle reels per episode on TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts |
| **Rating / tone** | All ages, aimed at 16–28. Cinematic, playful and optimistic, with real stakes but no cynicism |

**Why 8 minutes:** That's long enough to build characters people care about, short enough to finish in one
sitting on a phone, and the whole season is shorter than a feature film, so it's easy to binge.

## Season arc (one sentence each)
- **Setup (Ep 1–2):** Vee just wants to win; the Flicker is somebody else's problem.
- **Quest (Ep 3–4):** Following the glitch takes Vee and Pix through districts they've never
  cared about, and each one shows them how much depends on the Current.
- **Twist (Ep 5):** The Flicker isn't an attack. The districts have been cut off from each other.
- **Payoff (Ep 6):** Every district reconnects at once, during the Zenith Open finals.

## Recurring devices
- **Pix's "What's that?"** Every episode Pix asks about one thing in the city, and the answer is
  quietly a real AMD use case. These double as standalone Shorts.
- **The zoom-in.** Each episode has one Roku City-style shot where the camera dives into a device
  (a console, a laptop, a scanner) and finds a living world inside.
- **Hidden logos.** Every episode hides the AMD logo in 3–5 places on the infrastructure. Fans hunt for them in the comments,
  which drives rewatches. A "logo count" reveal reel posts after each episode.
- **Kit's film.** Kit's short film runs through the season as a B-story and premieres in Ep 6. It's the
  hook for a student animation contest (see `05-audience-cta-distribution-metrics.md`).

## How AMD shows up (and doesn't)

| ✅ Do | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|
| Logo on substations, racks, scoreboards, pod dashboards | Characters saying "AMD" or naming product SKUs |
| Each district clearly tied to a real sector | Spec sheets, benchmarks or performance claims in-show |
| End-card: *"Zenith runs on AMD. Find the power →"* | A narrator explaining the technology |
| Product connections in the interactive map and the reel captions | Product beauty shots cut into the story |
