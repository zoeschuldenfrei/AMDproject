The New Concept

SUBSTRATE

A gothic-futuristic city whose people have no idea what their world really is. Every reel ends with the camera pulling back until we realize the entire city is built on a processor.

THE SKYLINE

is circuitry

Towers are the chip’s architecture.

→
THE RIVERS OF LIGHT

are data

Photons pouring between the spires.

→
THE PULLBACK

reveals the chip

…and the AMD logo. The brand reveal is the payoff.

The World

Two cities. One chip.

The Spires

The new city above. Light moves instead of electricity: clean cyan rivers of photons pour between glass towers. Impossible speed.

The Underlayer

The old city on aging legacy silicon. Sickly green fog, bridges over chasms, everything hot and slow. Scrappy, inventive, stuck.

Ren

An Underlayer courier who can see the current: the light and data no one else notices.

The Seed

A stained-glass sphere: a new core being born. Where it’s placed decides which half of the city grows.

The Gate

A vast ring vault at the city’s center, secretly a data center, where the Seed must be delivered.
